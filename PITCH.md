# SevaSetu — Institutional Pitch & Executive Presentation Deck

> **"Transforming fragmented civic document verification into an accountable, trackable, human-in-the-loop digital case lifecycle."**

---

## 1. The Executive Problem

Across India's administrative tehsils, district collectorates, and municipal service centers, document verification remains the single largest bottleneck in public service delivery:

- **15 to 30 Days Average Turnaround**: Citizens wait weeks for simple income, caste, domicile, or scheme approvals.
- **High Counter Revisit Rates**: Over 35% of citizen applications require multiple counter visits due to unannounced scan blur, wrong document slots, or minor name spelling discrepancies.
- **Officer Burnout**: Frontline revenue staff spend 60%+ of their working hours on routine clerical cross-checking rather than substantive case evaluations.
- **Unverifiable Paper Decisions**: Certificates and rejection slips lack tamper-evident digital verification, making third-party authenticity checking cumbersome.

---

## 2. The SevaSetu Solution

SevaSetu provides a **complete, government-grade civic document pre-verification and case-management infrastructure**:

1. **Immediate Frontline Screening**: Analyzes citizen document uploads in real time (OCR text extraction, Laplacian blur scoring, and document type classification).
2. **Explainable AI Decision Support**: Reconciles demographic tokens (Name, DOB, Address, Gender) across all uploaded proofs and highlights discrepancies for human review.
3. **Statutory Interview Gate**: Interactive, grounded confirmation session (browser video/audio + text fallback) ensuring verified citizen intent.
4. **Structured Officer Case Workbench**: Two-tier officer queue with statutory SLA monitoring, side-by-side evidence inspection, and structured correction workflows.
5. **Tamper-Evident Decisions & Public Verification**: Issues cryptographic decision certificates with embedded QR codes verified independently at `/verify` without citizen login.
6. **Integrated Grievance Redressal**: Built-in 8-state escalation workflow ensuring citizens have an accountable mechanism to challenge determinations.

## 3. The 3 Core Journeys

### Journey A: The Citizen Experience
- Discovers 84 statutory government service requirement rules with official source provenance.
- Uploads documents with immediate feedback on scan readability and document slot matches.
- Receives transparent status notifications, participates in the verification interview, and downloads official decision certificates.
- Can lodge auditable grievances with SLA tracking if processing delays occur.

### Journey B: The Officer Experience
- Inspects applications prioritized by readiness score and statutory SLA countdowns.
- Reviews advisory AI findings side-by-side with original document evidence.
- Initiates structured correction requests (allowing citizen resubmission without queue loss) or unlocks the statutory interview gate.
- Authorizes approvals or rejections backed by formal evidentiary records.

### Journey C: The Executive & Administration
- Monitors district-wide throughput via the **Executive Command Center (`/command-center`)**.
- Observes real-time SLA compliance percentages, officer workload distributions, and grievance resolution rates.
- Enforces multi-role RBAC, immutable SHA-256 audit ledgers, and purpose-bound privacy consent policies.

---

## 4. Responsible AI & Statutory Authority

SevaSetu adheres strictly to modern e-Governance ethical AI principles:

> **"AI assists verification. Final statutory decisions remain with authorized government officers."**

- **What AI Does**: Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Laplacian blur quality assessment, cross-document demographic reconciliation, duplicate detection, and advisory readiness scoring.
- **What AI Never Does**: Never autonomously approves applications, never autonomously rejects citizens, never uses facial/emotion recognition, and never overrides officer discretion.
